5. RC Time Constant Reference

Time Charging Level Remaining During Discharge Interpretation

The 3τ–5τ interval is commonly treated as the practical settling region: approximately 95.0% to 99.3% charged, or 5.0% to 0.7% remaining during discharge. The time-domain plot shows both τ-based ticks and the corresponding real time values.
